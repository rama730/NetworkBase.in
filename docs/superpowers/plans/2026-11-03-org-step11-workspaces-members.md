# Step 11 — Workspaces and Members

Report: 7.9, 7.10, 17.9–17.11, 24.3, 25.1–25.8, 26.4, 26.5, 26.8, 28.6–28.8, 29.4, 29.6, 30.7, 30.8, 31.4, 32.4, 33.5, 35, 36, 42.10.
Built in four commits.

## 11a — Workspaces tab and a workspace's life (35.3–35.5)
- Archive records who did it and always tells the team; **Unarchive** by whoever could archive it (an admin's archive only by an admin).
- **Assign lead** only for a workspace flagged with no lead: team members first, then anyone in the organization; never Viewers, outside people or blocked people. The holder steps back to admin access; group work moves.
- Rows: open seats with Apply and a Has open seats filter; ⋯ Archive, Unarchive, Detach, Assign lead; who archived it.
- GitHub status line for the team (24.4).

## 11b — Teams (25.3–25.8, 42.10)
- Seats 1–10 per team, never below the people in it.
- Move to another workspace (organization admins): open tasks in the old one are unassigned; the old lead hears only "1 seat open".
- Ask to change team becomes a Members → Requests row with Move and Dismiss.
- Moving someone between teams offers to move their open team tasks (problem 17, decision 138).
- Bulk Add checks seats first; team mentions `@Frontend · 4`.

## 11c — Members (28.7, 31.4, 36.2–36.5, 26.5, 26.8)
- Role dialog: a preview of what changes (copies, groups, #general, teams), refused when a Viewer-to-be leads a workspace.
- Role changes act on copies: becoming Admin or Viewer removes unsubmitted copies; becoming Member gets open assignments.
- Outside: Remove from workspace, lead told. Former: who ended it, how, when and why; Invite back, Unblock.
- Remove dialog with consequences; Verified filter and bulk Verify; join request notes.
