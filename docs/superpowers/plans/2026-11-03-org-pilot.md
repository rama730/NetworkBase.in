## 1. Before the deploy (owner)

1. **The count (44.4).** Run the read-only query in 44.4 in the Supabase SQL editor. Real classes running means: owner notices after each fix stage, and the classic-board switch is ready.
2. **Resend (decision 164).** The free plan is 100 emails a UTC day and 3,000 a month, counted per recipient ([quotas](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/account-quotas-and-limits)). A class of 60 sends:
   | Day | Emails |
   |---|---|
   | Joining day | 60 welcomes, plus sign-up confirmations if Supabase sends them through Resend |
   | Publishing an assignment | 60 |
   | Due soon (per assignment) | up to 60, or 120 with "both" reminders |
   | An emailed announcement | 60 (the app allows 5 a day) |

   Two weeks come to about 550–650, inside the month, but any day with two of these rows passes 100, and over the cap emails fail until midnight UTC (the tray still works). **Buy Pro before the pilot for a class over about 40**, or keep publishing, announcements and due dates on different days.

## 3. Signed-in checks before inviting the class

Owner account and one test student account, two browsers:
1. Create an organization, invite by code, the student joins and sees the welcome panel.
2. Publish an assignment with review; the student hands in a file; the owner approves in the queue (A/R keys), returns once with Fix by.
3. A team assignment: auto-make groups, the lead hands in.
4. Preview as member: the bar shows, buttons are refused, Exit works.
5. Search as the student: no draft documents, no one else's copy.

## 4. The pilot (44.7)

- One college class of 30 to 60, a teacher who reports problems, two weeks, one full sprint, two assignments (one with review, one with teams).
- Teacher creates the organization; students join by code.
- Daily: Sentry new issues; the tray works; email outbox has nothing older than an hour (last query in `scripts/sql/org-measures.sql`).
- Weekly: Inngest executions, Resend usage, `scripts/sql/org-measures.sql`.
- Stop at once on any privacy problem or lost work.
- Finish: no privacy or data-loss bugs, every serious bug fixed, the teacher would keep using it.
