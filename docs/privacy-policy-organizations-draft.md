## Organizations

An organization is a shared space run by its owner and admins, such as a college class, a company team or a club. Joining one shares some of your activity there with the people who run it.

### What an organization's owner and admins can see

| They can see | They can't see |
|---|---|
| Your name, role, title and the date you joined | Your direct messages |
| When you were last active in that organization | Your activity outside that organization |
| The status of your assignments, your submissions and their files | Other organizations or your personal workspaces |
| Private conversations on your assignments | Your email address, unless they invited you by email |
| Your recent activity in that organization and its workspaces | Your block list |
| Whether you have seen announcements and read documents | Who reported something |
| Who downloaded a file, kept for 90 days | Your contribution drafts |
| Channels in the organization, including a workspace's channels if they open them | |

"Last active" means the last time you opened or did something in that organization or its workspaces. It is updated at most once an hour. It is not your activity across NetworkBase.

### What happens to your data

| Data | After you leave or are removed | After the organization is deleted |
|---|---|---|
| Submissions | Kept for the organization's admins; you lose access | You can download them for 30 days, then they are deleted |
| Assignments you hadn't handed in | Removed | Deleted |
| Group work | Stays with the group | Deleted, except workspaces their owners chose to keep |
| Posts, comments and files | Stay, with your name | Deleted after 30 days |
| Channel messages | Stay | Deleted after 30 days |
| Seen, read and activity history | Kept for the admins | Deleted after 30 days |
| Download history | Kept for 90 days | Deleted after 30 days |
| Your profile entry for the organization | Follows your profile settings | Keeps the organization's name, with no link |
| If you delete your account | Your own copies of assignments are deleted; group work shows "Deleted user" | Same |

An archived organization keeps everything until its owner deletes it. Nothing expires on its own while it is archived.

### Your data export

Your account export includes your own submissions and your organization memberships.

### Age

NetworkBase is for people aged 18 or older, including in organizations. Organizations for school classes are not offered.
